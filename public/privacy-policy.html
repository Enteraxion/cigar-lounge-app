Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Reserve ("the App," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application for discovering, reviewing, and saving cigar lounges.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password (managed securely via Firebase Authentication) when you create an account.
  • Profile information: optional details you choose to add, such as a profile photo, home city, favorite cigar brand, or favorite lounge.
  • Content you create: reviews, star ratings, and photos you submit for lounges, as well as collections you build and favorites/lounges you save.
  • Location information: with your permission, we use your device's GPS location to show nearby lounges and sort search results by distance. Location is used to power these features in the moment and is not stored on our servers beyond what's needed to display results to you.
  • Usage information: your search history and recently viewed lounges within the app, which power features like search suggestions and your personal browsing history.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To show you relevant lounges based on your location and search activity
  • To display your reviews, ratings, and photos to other users of the app
  • To let you save favorites, build collections, and revisit lounges you've viewed before
  • To notify you of activity relevant to you within the app (e.g., a reply to your review)

Information Visible to Other Users

Your display name, profile photo, and any reviews, ratings, or photos you submit are visible to other users of the app. Your email address, search history, favorites, and saved collections are private and not shown to other users.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Firebase (Google): authentication, database storage, file/photo storage, and backend functions.
  • Yelp Fusion API: used server-side to source public business listing information (lounge names, addresses, hours, and photos) for lounges shown in the app. This is public business data, not personal data about you.

Data Retention & Deletion

You can edit or delete your reviews and update your profile at any time within the app. To request full deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
