Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This policy explains what Lounge Locator (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted. Lounge Locator is a directory for discovering, reviewing and reserving tables at cigar lounges. It is operated by Enteraxion LLC.

Lounge Locator is for adults aged 21 and over. To use the parts of the app that involve reviewing, reserving or claiming a business, we ask you to prove your age by photographing an identity document. That is the most sensitive information we hold, and the section below explains exactly what happens to it.

Age verification and identity documents

Because this is a 21+ app, we ask you to verify your age. You choose which document to use — a driver’s licence, a state ID card, a passport or a military ID — and photograph it in the app.

What we collect

  • Photographs of your identity document. For a card we ask for both sides, because the date of birth is on the front and the security features are on the back. For a passport we ask only for the photo page.
  • Which type of document you used, and the date of birth you entered when creating your account.
  • The outcome of the review — verified or not — and when it was decided.

Who sees it, and for how long

  • An automated check reads it first, and a person reviews anything it cannot settle. Your photographs are sent to Microsoft Azure OpenAI, which reads the printed date of birth and compares it against what you entered. If everything agrees and the document is in date, you are verified without a person seeing it. If anything does not add up — the photo is unclear, the dates disagree, the document has expired — an administrator at Enteraxion opens the photographs and decides.
  • Microsoft does not keep them. The images are sent for that single check and are not used to train any model. We do not send your name, email address or account details with them.
  • They are stored privately in Firebase Storage, under access rules that permit only you and an administrator to read them. They are never shown to other members and never attached to your reviews or profile.
  • They are deleted when you delete your account, along with everything else described below. You can do that yourself, in the app, at any time — see “Deleting your account”.

What we do not do with it

  • We do not sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.
  • We do not run facial recognition on it, and neither does the automated check — it reads the printed text, it does not identify your face.
  • We do not use it for anything other than confirming you are 21 or over.

What other members can see

Your display name, profile photo, and any reviews, ratings and photos you post are visible to other members. That is the point of a review.

Everything else is not. Your email address, date of birth, identity documents, reservations, search history, favourites, collections and problem reports are private. Other members cannot see them, and our security rules are written to prevent it rather than merely discourage it.

If you claim a business

When you claim a lounge as its owner, the contact details you submit are sent to our sales team so they can get in touch, and the claim itself is reviewed by an administrator before ownership is granted. Once you own a listing, the details you publish on it — description, opening hours, amenities, stock — are public, because that is the listing members see.

Services we use

  • Firebase (Google) — accounts, database, file storage, and the server-side functions that run the app.
  • Yelp and Google Places — used server-side to source public business information about lounges: names, addresses, opening hours and photographs. This is public data about businesses, not personal data about you.
  • SendGrid (Twilio) — sends the emails the app needs to send, such as your verification code and reservation notifications.
  • Apple speech recognition — used only when you tap the microphone to search by voice. The audio may be transcribed on Apple's servers rather than on your device. Nothing is sent unless you start a voice search.
  • National Weather Service — we send an approximate map location to retrieve current conditions. No account details are sent with it.
  • Microsoft Azure OpenAI — two things. It powers the in-app Concierge, which receives your question and a list of lounges we have selected in order to recommend from it. And it performs the automated first check on your identity document, described above.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself, from inside the app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. You do not need to email anyone or ask permission.

Deleting removes your account, your reviews and their photographs, your reservations, your saved lounges and collections, your problem reports, and the photographs of your identity document. If you own a lounge listing, the listing stays in the directory but is released, so it can be claimed again. This cannot be undone.

Keeping it secure

Data is stored with Firebase and protected by security rules that define exactly who can read and write each piece of information. Those rules are tested automatically as part of our build, including tests that confirm one member cannot read another member’s reservations or identity documents.

No system is perfect. If you believe your account has been accessed by someone else, contact us at the address below.

Children

Lounge Locator is not for anyone under 21, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under that age. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will be brought to your attention in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about your data, can be sent to admin@enteraxion.com.
